Purpose

Welcome to my Fort Worth guide! This project highlights some of my favorite local coffee shops, restaurants, and places to visit around the city. My goal is to create one big review and travel guide that feels helpful, personal, and easy to explore. I want to give honest reviews and suggestions that are based off of taste, atmosphere and affordability! I hope you enjoy my guide to all things food and drink in fort worth!!

About Me

I am a 22 year old senior at TCU that has tried several amazing spots over the past four years across all parts of Fort Worth. The reviews will include not only my personal favorites but includes the results of a survey given out to my TCU friends and peers. I can't wait to share my personal go-to spots, my friends favorites and all my visitors over the past few year's top picks!
