Research

Overview

This project looks at how college students and young adults find and choose restaurants and coffee shops in Fort Worth. Instead of relying only on large review platforms, I wanted to create something more curated, personal, and actually helpful.

My research combines academic studies, local guides, and digital platforms to better understand what people are looking for.

How People Choose Where to Eat

Research on young adults’ food habits shows that convenience, affordability, and social environments play a major role in where people go.

See: Powell et al., “Food Choices of Young Adults in the United States of America: A Scoping Review.”

Most decisions are based on lifestyle, not just the food itself. Because of this, my site focuses on places that are:

  • easy to get to
  • affordable for students
  • good for social settings like studying or going out

Reviews & Digital Platforms

Platforms like Yelp and Tripadvisor play a major role in how people discover restaurants.

Online reviews and ratings strongly influence decision making, especially when users are comparing multiple options.

See: BrightLocal’s Local Consumer Review Survey.

While helpful, these platforms can feel overwhelming and not very personal. This project simplifies that experience by offering a more curated and easy-to-navigate guide.

Coffee Culture & Atmosphere

Coffee shop recommendations often focus on more than just taste. Sources like Abroad with Ash and A Cowboys Life highlight how important the overall experience is.

Things that matter most include:

  • atmosphere
  • seating and layout
  • study friendliness
  • overall vibe

These ideas directly influenced how I reviewed and categorized coffee shops on this site.

Writing Style & Reviews

To shape my writing style, I used guidance from Eater.

Good reviews focus on being specific, honest, and actually helpful to other people. Because of this, my reviews are designed to feel personal and conversational, while still giving useful details someone could rely on.

Fort Worth Food Culture

To better understand the local scene, I looked at Visit Fort Worth and Fort Worth Magazine.

Fort Worth’s food scene is a mix of:

  • traditional staples like Tex-Mex and barbecue
  • newer, trendier restaurants
  • a growing variety of cuisines and experiences

This helped shape how I organized my site and chose what to include.

Use of AI

AI tools, including ChatGPT, were used to help with brainstorming, editing, and organizing content.
