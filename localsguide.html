Locals Guide - TCU Students Share Their Favorites

☕ Ashley's Picks

By Ashley
Coffee

Match Point Coffee a cute, low-key place away from campus that's perfect when you want a calmer vibe.

Brunch

Press Cafe thanks to its indoor/outdoor seating and wide menu.

Lunch

Local Foods quick, healthy, and still really good.

Dinner

Paloma Suerte for some of the best Mexican food in Fort Worth and a fun atmosphere. Top recommendation for visitors.

Press Cafe for classic, reliable options.

Gusto's Burger casual, fast, and really good. Finds spots on TikTok & Instagram. Recommends Joe T. Garcia's or Bowie House for visitors.
